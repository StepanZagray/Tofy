# Planning in learned latent spaces with discrete/combinatorial actions — literature findings for Tofy

## 1. MuZero lineage and search-budget evidence

**Policy improvement by planning with Gumbel** (Danihelka, Guez, Schrittwieser, Silver; ICLR 2022, OpenReview `bERaNdoegnO` — no arXiv id). Root: sample m actions without replacement via Gumbel-Top-k over policy logits, run Sequential Halving over them, pick argmax of g(a)+logits(a)+σ(completed-Q); train policy toward "completed Q-values". Guarantees monotone policy improvement *for any simulation count* if Q estimates are correct — visit-count targets (vanilla MuZero) do not. Measured: matches vanilla MuZero/AlphaZero on Go/chess/Atari at large budgets and "significantly improves prior performance when planning with few simulations"; the paper's ablations go down to n=2–16 simulations where vanilla MuZero's learning collapses and Gumbel still improves. (I could not fetch the raw PDF — OpenReview bot-walled — so exact Elo deltas below come from independent replication.)

**MiniZero** (arXiv 2310.11305, Oct 2023; IEEE ToG 2024) — independent replication across 9x9 Go, 8x8 Othello, 57 Atari games: (a) more simulations generally better at equal *sample* count — at n=200 vanilla beats Gumbel n=2/n=16; (b) at equal *wall-clock*, Gumbel AlphaZero n=2 ≈ vanilla n=200 on Othello; (c) Atari per-game winners: MuZero n=50 best in 40/57, but Gumbel MuZero n=2 best in 13/57 and n=50 "does not consistently outperform n=18 and n=2"; (d) *progressive simulation* (growing n during training) beats fixed budgets on board games, mixed on Atari (25/57). Key takeaway: n=2–18 simulations is a genuinely workable operating point, especially per wall-clock.

**Sampled MuZero** (arXiv 2104.06303, Apr 2021, ICML). Plans over K sampled actions with corrected prior π̂_β=(β̂/β)π in PUCT. Ablations: Go (362 actions) K∈{15,25,50,100}: K=50 near-baseline, K=100 matches full enumeration; DM-Control humanoid: K=3 already learns, no gain past K=10. So sampling ~1–3% of a Go-sized action space suffices when the prior is decent.

**Stochastic MuZero** (Antonoglou et al., ICLR 2022, "Planning in Stochastic Environments with a Learned Model"): afterstates + discrete chance codes. ARC-AGI-3 games are deterministic → not needed for Tofy; only relevant if latent aliasing makes dynamics *appear* stochastic.

**Feasibility for Tofy:** this is the strongest-evidenced family. Gumbel-root search with m=16–64 Gumbel-sampled candidates from a policy prior over ~4000 actions, n=16–64 simulations, is exactly the regime the evidence covers, and with a 1M-param model one simulation ≈ one tiny forward pass; a full n=64 search is milliseconds on an A40 — far inside 0.1–2 s. Missing pieces in Tofy today: a policy prior and a value/objective head (see §4, §7).

## 2. 2024–2026 successors

**EfficientZero V2** (arXiv 2403.00564, Mar 2024, ICML 2024 spotlight). Uses *sampling-based Gumbel search* everywhere: Atari 100k config = **16 simulations, 8 sampled actions**; continuous = 32 sims/16 samples; two-portion sampling (policy + flattened prior) for exploration. Their ablation: their search with **8 simulations beats Sample-MCTS with 50**. The comparison table lists a planning net far smaller than TD-MPC2's 4.9M (extracted as ~1.3M) — i.e., Tofy-scale models plus tiny Gumbel search is a proven combination. Beats DreamerV3 on 50/66 tasks. Also note EfficientZero's temporal-consistency loss (SimSiam-style latent prediction) is essentially a JEPA objective inside a MuZero — the closest existing "MuZero-with-JEPA-encoder" precedent.

**UniZero** (arXiv 2406.10667, Jun 2024, ICLR 2025). Transformer (nanoGPT) world model, **decoder-free** (explicitly no reconstruction; SimNorm latents), joint latent-dynamics + value/policy training, MCTS on top. Beats MuZero (stack4) in 17/26 Atari-100k games with single-frame input; big wins on memory tasks. Direct evidence that a decoder-free latent world model supports MCTS — the closest published relative of "JEPA + MuZero search". Lives in **LightZero** (arXiv 2310.08348, NeurIPS 2023 benchmark), whose cross-algorithm results also show Gumbel variants excel "when possible actions greatly exceed the simulation budget" — exactly Tofy's 4000≫n case.

**ReZero** (arXiv 2404.16364, Apr 2024): backward-view reanalyze + entire-buffer reanalyze; boosts *training* wall-clock of any LightZero algorithm; orthogonal to per-action inference budget — relevant only if Tofy trains MCTS-in-the-loop.

**TransZero** (arXiv 2509.11233, Sep 2025): replaces sequential MCTS node-by-node expansion with transformer-parallelized batched tree expansion (builds on Gumbel/sequential-halving); large wall-clock speedups at equal returns on Atari/board games. Directly useful blueprint: Tofy's cheap batched one-step predictions are precisely what this exploits.

**Token/discrete world models used for imagination, not search:** Δ-IRIS (arXiv 2406.19320, ICML 2024; Crafter SOTA at multiple budgets, ~10× faster than IRIS) and DART (arXiv 2406.01361, ICML 2024; discrete tokens, Atari-100k median HNS 0.790 *without lookahead search*). Both learn policies in imagination — evidence that token WMs work at small scale, but neither does test-time search. The canonical token-WM-with-search is **Vector Quantized Models for Planning** (arXiv 2106.04615, ICML 2021): VQ-VAE state codes + MuZero-style MCTS over actions *and* discrete latent codes; beat offline MuZero on stochastic chess. For deterministic Tofy only the action branch is needed.

**"PiZero" (arXiv 2502.xxxx):** I could not find any such paper on arXiv or elsewhere (searches return only particle-physics π⁰ papers and an unrelated company). Verdict: likely a misremembered name — nearest real matches are TransZero (2509.11233) and UniZero. Do not cite it.

## 3. Beam/best-first search directly in latent space

Published evidence is thin — this is the genuine gap in the literature:
- **PLDM / "A Case for Planning with Latent Dynamics Models"** (Sobal, LeCun et al., arXiv 2502.14819, Feb 2025): JEPA latents + **MPPI** (not tree search) on top-down navigation; shows latent planning generalizes to novel layouts and stitches trajectories far better than model-free RL from the same reward-free offline data. Continuous relaxation of actions, small models — closest published relative of Tofy's whole stack.
- **DINO-WM** (arXiv 2411.04983, Nov 2024): frozen DINOv2 patch features + ViT dynamics; zero-shot MPC with **CEM** toward goal features (opt_steps≈30). No search; goal-latent-distance objective is the transferable idea.
- **"What Drives Success in Physical Planning with JEPA World Models?"** (arXiv 2512.24497, Dec 2025): systematic planner comparison over JEPA latents — **CEM > Nevergrad > gradient descent** (GD fails on navigation-like non-smooth landscapes); multistep (k-step) rollout training and ≥2-frame context are what make planning work; ViT-S + depth-6 predictor suffices for sim tasks. Directly actionable training advice for Tofy (train with k-step latent rollout losses).
- Best-first/A*-style search over *learned latent* models appears mainly in classical-planning hybrids (learned GNN heuristics guiding best-first search, e.g. arXiv 2605.25720 and Bai et al. 2025 symmetry-pruned best-first search), not over JEPA latents. **No published "latent beam search" method with strong evidence exists** — but nothing forbids it: with deterministic dynamics and batched expansion, beam search is just breadth-limited best-first; the MuZero literature's implicit warning is that raw value-greedy expansion without visit-based uncertainty handling (what MCTS provides) compounds value-net errors.

## 4. Huge factored/coordinate action spaces (the 64×64 click)

- **Sampled MuZero** (above): K=15–50 samples of 362 Go actions ≈ full enumeration → sampling ~50–200 of Tofy's 4000 actions from a prior should suffice.
- **AlphaZero-style spatial priors**: the canonical solution to coordinate actions — AlphaZero already handles 362 (Go) and 4672 (chess 8×8×73 planes) discrete actions with a *spatial policy head* + PUCT; Tofy's natural head is 7 type logits + a 64×64 logit map for ACTION6 (factored as type × coordinate). This is standard and battle-tested; progressive widening (Coulom 2007; double progressive widening, Couëtoux et al. 2011) is the pre-deep-learning tool and has been effectively superseded by prior-guided PUCT/Gumbel-Top-k for discrete spaces.
- **Multiagent Gumbel MuZero** (AAAI 2024, Xu et al.): factors a combinatorial joint action into sequential per-dimension decisions inside the Gumbel tree — the pattern to copy if you want to pick action-type then coordinate as two tree levels instead of 4000 flat arms.
- **DLPA / Model-based RL for Parameterized Action Spaces** (arXiv 2404.03037, Apr 2024): MPPI adapted to discrete-action-plus-continuous-parameter spaces by maintaining a separate parameter distribution per discrete action — structurally *exactly* ACTION6(x,y) if you relax the coordinate to continuous, then round.
- **One-step best-of-k / argmin-energy**: no dedicated paper, but it is the degenerate n=1-depth case of everything above and what DINO-WM/PLDM reduce to at horizon 1; with Tofy's batching you can *exhaustively* score all 4000 actions one step deep (one batch, milliseconds) — cheaper than any sampling scheme at depth 1. Literature only becomes relevant at depth ≥ 2 (4000² blows up → priors/sampling needed).

## 5. CEM/MPPI for categorical actions

Published evidence exists but is sparse:
- **Smolyanskiy, LunarLander RSSM study** (arXiv 2607.01736, Jul 2026): concrete, working **discrete CEM** recipe on 4 discrete actions: per-step categorical distribution over horizon H=25; each iteration sample N=384 sequences, roll open-loop through the RSSM, keep E=48 elites, update per-step logits toward elite action frequencies with smoothing α=0.7, I=4 iterations, execute first action, re-plan. Drives a strong zero-shot CEM-MPC policy (smoothed return +153 at the best checkpoint). Also a warning highly relevant to Tofy: validation loss and multi-step RMSE keep improving *after closed-loop planning performance collapses*; their reward-observability diagnostics (ROF/CROF) are the fix.
- MPPI-over-softmax-logits: no strong standalone publication found; DLPA (2404.03037) is the nearest published MPPI-with-discrete-components evidence.
- Verdict for Tofy: categorical CEM is implementable and proven at 4 actions, but at 4000-way categoricals per step the elite-frequency estimates get extremely noisy unless N is huge; with sparse deterministic puzzle rewards, population methods waste most samples. Use it only with a strong prior initializing the logits — at which point Gumbel/sequential-halving does the same job with an improvement guarantee.

## 6. TD-MPC2 and discrete adaptations

**TD-MPC2** (arXiv 2310.16828, Oct 2023, ICLR 2024): decoder-free (JEPA-like latent-consistency) world model + MPPI + value; 5M-param default; continuous control only. The authors *explicitly* list discrete actions as an open problem and suggest replacing MPPI with MCTS (MuZero-style) — i.e., the field's own answer to "CEM/MPPI assume continuous actions, what instead?" is §1/§2 of this report. No published discrete TD-MPC variant with evidence surfaced for 2024–26; the discrete-codebook line (Discrete Codebook World Models, arXiv 2503.00653, ICLR 2025) discretizes the *latent*, not the action.

## 7. Conclusions for Tofy

1. **Primary recommendation — Gumbel-root search:** add (a) a policy prior head: 7 type logits + 64×64 coordinate map (AlphaZero spatial head), (b) a value/objective signal, then plan with Gumbel-Top-k sampling of m=16–64 root candidates + Sequential Halving, depth 2–5, n=32–128 simulations. Evidence base: Gumbel paper (improvement guarantee at any n), MiniZero (n=2–18 workable), EZ-V2 (16 sims/8 samples beats 50-sim Sample-MCTS *at ~1M-param scale*), LightZero (Gumbel best when |A|≫n). Cost: with batched 1M-param one-step latents, even n=128 is ≪100 ms — comfortably inside 0.1–2 s; TransZero shows how to batch expansion if depth grows.
2. **Exploit determinism + cheap batching:** at depth 1, skip sampling entirely — score *all* ~4000 actions in one batch (one-step best-of-k is free); use the prior only to prune from depth 2 onward. A deduplicating batched best-first/beam variant is unpublished territory but low-risk given determinism; MCTS-style visit statistics are only needed to hedge value-net error.
3. **Objective is the real gap, not the search:** Tofy has no reward/value head; every working system above plans against either a learned value (MuZero family) or goal-latent distance (DINO-WM, PLDM, 2512.24497). For ARC-AGI-3's sparse score changes, start with goal/novelty distance in latent space plus a small learned value.
4. **Training changes that the JEPA-planning literature says matter:** k-step open-loop rollout losses (2512.24497: optimal k≈2–6), ≥2-frame context, and closed-loop-aware checkpoint selection (2607.01736: prediction metrics keep improving after planability collapses — resonates with the collapse+freeze diagnosis already in Tofy's research memory).
5. **Skip:** Stochastic MuZero (deterministic games), continuous CEM/MPPI relaxations (dominated by Gumbel search here), progressive widening (superseded by priors), and "PiZero" (does not exist under that name).
