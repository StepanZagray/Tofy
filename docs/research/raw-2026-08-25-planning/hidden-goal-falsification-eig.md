Research complete. Findings below, organized by topic, with feasibility calls for Tofy (~1M-param action-conditioned JEPA, 64×64 grids, ~4000 discrete actions, 0.1–2 s/action, no LLMs).

# Literature review: planning under unknown goals/rules

## Headline conclusion

"Planning by falsification" — choosing actions whose predicted outcomes best discriminate competing hypotheses — is exactly the **Expected Information Gain (EIG) / Bayesian Optimal Experimental Design (BOED) objective applied to a hypothesis set over world rules**. Its closest living relatives, ranked by proximity:

1. **OPINE-World** (ARC-AGI-3 itself, Bayesian error-prioritized exploration over model hypotheses) — closest in domain and spirit, but LLM-based.
2. **Plan2Explore lineage** (ensemble disagreement as a tractable EIG proxy, used *inside planning*) — closest in mechanism that actually fits Tofy's compute budget.
3. **DAD/Step-DAD amortized BED** — closest in objective formalism (sequential EIG policies at millisecond latency).
4. **WorldCoder / CWM-GGP / WorldLLM / Model Discovery Agent** — closest in "maintain candidate rule programs, act to refute them" framing, all LLM-dependent.
5. **VariBAD/BAMDP** — closest in "plan against a posterior over the unknown task" framing; goal inference falls out of Bayes-optimal reward maximization rather than an explicit falsification score.

No paper found uses literally "planning by falsification" as a named method; the idea is distributed across these families, and the strongest quantitative evidence that discrimination-driven action selection beats greedy/random is in the BED and Plan2Explore lineages.

## A. Bayesian experimental design / EIG action selection (2024–26 + roots)

**DAD — Deep Adaptive Design** ([arXiv 2103.02438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.02438), ICML 2021, Foster et al.) — canonical root. Objective: total sequential EIG, lower-bounded by the sequential Prior Contrastive Estimation (sPCE) bound; a design policy network is trained offline so each experiment choice is one forward pass (milliseconds). Hypotheses: explicit parameter prior + contrastive samples. Evidence: higher total EIG and posterior accuracy than myopic/greedy BED at a fraction of deployment cost.

**Step-DAD — Semi-Amortized Policy-Based BED** ([arXiv 2507.14057](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.14057), ICML 2025) — periodically fine-tunes the DAD policy on the actual posterior mid-deployment. Evidence: improved EIG and robustness vs DAD and fully amortized baselines. Shows the amortize-then-refine pattern works.

**ACED — Adaptive Causal Experimental Design** ([OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=JPnq9wSuSG)) — amortized non-myopic EIG policies for causal-quantity targets; same family, causal-graph hypotheses.

**PTS-BE — Efficient Bayesian Exploration in Model-Based RL** ([arXiv 2507.02639](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02639), Caron, Hicks, Mavroudis, July 2025) — the most direct recent "EIG as planning bonus in MBRL" paper. Objective: information-gain bonus targeting epistemic (not aleatoric) uncertainty, with proofs that the bonus vanishes as the model is learned; posterior via sparse variational GPs, deep kernels, or deep ensembles; IG evaluated over *sampled candidate trajectories* during planning (predictive trajectory sampling). Evidence: substantially outperforms baselines on sparse-reward and pure-exploration tasks. Directly matches Tofy's shape: ensemble + short rollouts + pick trajectory maximizing IG.

**MaxInfoRL** ([arXiv 2412.12098](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.12098v1), Sukhija, Coros, Krause, Abbeel, Sferrazza, Dec 2024) — combines extrinsic value with intrinsic information gain about dynamics via Boltzmann trade-off; sublinear regret in bandit setting; strong results on hard-exploration visual control. Bonus-style rather than discriminative planning, but gives a principled extrinsic/epistemic mixing rule Tofy could reuse for the action-efficiency metric.

**CIG — Exploration via Conditional Information Gain** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.20878), NeurIPS) — refines ensemble-disagreement IG by conditioning to strip aleatoric noise; compares directly against Plan2Explore-style disagreement.

**Expected Free Energy planning as variational inference** ([arXiv 2504.14898](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14898) / [2606.20658](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20658), 2025–26) — active inference: EFE = expected utility + epistemic value (ambiguity/novelty), derived from a single variational objective with preference and epistemic priors. This is the cleanest *unified* form of "seek goal + falsify hypotheses" in one planning cost; scalability historically poor, but this line makes it a rollout-scoring functional compatible with a latent world model.

**Feasibility for Tofy:** high. All the discrete-EIG math reduces, with an ensemble of K tiny JEPA heads, to "score candidate action(-sequence)s by predicted ensemble disagreement / mutual information, mix with predicted goal value." K=4–8 heads × short rollouts over a pruned action set is well within 0.1–2 s. The DAD lesson (amortize the design policy; refine occasionally per Step-DAD) maps to training a small policy head that proposes discriminating actions, with explicit EIG scoring only over the top few.

## B. Hypothesis-elimination / version-space / program-induction planners

**WorldCoder** ([arXiv 2402.12275](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12275), Tang, Key, Ellis, NeurIPS 2024) — canonical hypothesis-testing loop: world model = Python program; maintain it to (i) explain all observed transitions and (ii) remain *optimistic* (a logical constraint that some plan achieves reward — "REx" refinement). Rules falsified by transitions that contradict the program trigger LLM rewrites. Evidence: orders-of-magnitude fewer environment interactions than deep RL on Sokoban/Minigrid, transfer across variants. Action selection is optimism-driven, not EIG-driven — falsification happens passively when optimistic plans fail. LLM-dependent; not runnable in Tofy's constraints, but the *optimism-under-hypotheses* trick (plan as if the goal-favorable hypothesis is true; its failure is itself informative) is implementable with JEPA hypotheses.

**Code World Models for General Game Playing** ([arXiv 2510.04542](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.04542), Lehrach, Hennes, Lazaro-Gredilla et al., ICLR 2026) — population of candidate code world models in a tree; **Thompson sampling over hypotheses** decides which candidate to refine; verified CWM then drives MCTS. Evidence: outperforms/matches Gemini 2.5 Pro on 9/10 games including imperfect information. Version-space maintenance + bandit allocation over hypotheses is the transferable idea.

**OPINE-World** ([arXiv 2607.01531](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.01531), Courtis, Li, Sanner, July 2026) — the single closest paper to the user's method *on the target benchmark*. Two agents (explorer + code synthesizer with CEGIS/counterexample-guided refinement); exploration steered by a **Bayesian "ontology error"** — actions chosen to reduce uncertainty about the object-type vocabulary itself, i.e., error-prioritized information seeking rather than random probing. Evidence: **solves 20/25 ARC-AGI-3 games with no per-game training, action-efficiency 78.4 vs human baseline**. LLM-based, but demonstrates that targeted uncertainty-reduction exploration is what buys action efficiency on this exact benchmark.

**WorldLLM** ([arXiv 2506.06725](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.06725), Levy, Colas, Oudeyer, Carta, Romac, 2025/26) — Scientist/Statistician/Experimenter loop: hypotheses = natural-language rules; Metropolis over hypotheses scored by transition likelihood; **experimenter is an RL policy rewarded for transitions with low likelihood under current hypotheses** (i.e., actively seeks falsifying evidence — the closest explicit "collect evidence against the current theory" objective found). Evidence: on Playground-Text, low-log-P-seeking policy reaches near-oracle coverage (0.93) where learning-progress variants fail; retains generalization where fine-tuning collapses. The *reward = negative likelihood under current hypotheses* signal is directly portable to a JEPA: reward = prediction error of current model, evaluated in imagination.

**Model Discovery Agent** ([arXiv 2608.09696](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.09696), Aug 2026) — LLM proposes mechanistic hypotheses, then explicit **Bayesian experiment design selects interventions that discriminate between competing hypotheses** — literal planning-by-falsification for scientific models. Very recent; evidence is data-efficiency of discovery vs non-designed experimentation.

**AutumnSynth / Autumn** ([POPL 2023](https://www.basis.ai/publications/autumn_popl23.pdf), Das, Tenenbaum, Solar-Lezama, Tavares; [Basis blog](https://www.basis.ai/blog/autumn/)) — synthesizes reactive grid-game programs (update functions + latent automata) from observation traces; 27/30 benchmark games recovered. Passive induction (no action selection), but the DSL/decomposition (perception → tracking → update-function synthesis → event synthesis) is the best-studied hypothesis space for exactly Tofy's game class. Follow-up: **WorldTest/AutumnBench** ([arXiv 2510.19788](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.19788), Tavares' group + Tenenbaum, Ellis, Oct 2025) — 43 interactive grid environments, 129 tasks; 517 humans vs 5 frontier models; **humans substantially outperform, gap attributed specifically to exploration and belief updating** — direct evidence that active experimentation is the bottleneck capability, not prediction.

**EMPA — theory-based RL** ([arXiv 2107.12544](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.12544), Tsividis et al.; journal version [Phil Trans Roy Soc A, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2024.0529)) — Bayesian inference over probabilistic *game-engine programs*; exploration is object-based and hypothesis-directed; matches human learning curves on 90 Atari-style games (minutes of play). The 2026 journal version is the mature statement. Closest cognitive-science ancestor of the falsification framing; evidence = human-level sample efficiency, not regret bounds.

**Feasibility for Tofy:** program hypotheses per se need synthesis machinery (LLM or DSL search) that likely violates the 0.1–2 s budget unless the DSL is very small; but a *finite version space* of parametric rule/goal hypotheses (e.g., candidate goal predicates over grid features, candidate object-interaction rules) with likelihood tracking + Thompson/EIG action selection is cheap and captures the same structure.

## C. Empowerment / information-theoretic objectives as planning costs

**Plan2Explore** ([arXiv 2005.05960](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.05960), Sekar et al., ICML 2020) — canonical root: EIG ≈ variance-of-ensemble-means over one-step latent predictions; crucially the disagreement is *planned toward in imagination* (a value function over future disagreement), not just a bonus. Evidence: zero-shot/few-shot adaptation close to reward-supervised Dreamer on DM-Control.

Descendants 2024–26: **DreamerV3-XP** ([arXiv 2510.21418](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21418v1), 2025) — disagreement over an ensemble of *reward* heads (uncertainty about what is rewarding — interesting for hidden-goal settings); **PROWL** ([arXiv 2605.18803](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18803v2), 2026) — regret-driven prioritization for world-model learning; **CIG** (above). **Epistemically-guided forward-backward exploration** ([arXiv 2507.05477](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.05477), 2025) — epistemic-uncertainty-directed exploration for reward-free FB pretraining.

Empowerment proper: 2024–26 output is thin and mostly skill-discovery ("Empowerment via Causal Learning"; process empowerment, [IOP 2025](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2632-072X/adf2ec)); empowerment measures control, not hypothesis discrimination — weak fit for goal inference; deprioritize.

**Feasibility for Tofy: this is the practical implementation of planning-by-falsification at 1M params.** Ensemble-mean-variance in JEPA latent space is a few extra prediction heads; scoring ~tens of candidate actions × short rollouts is trivially within budget. The known caveat (disagreement conflates aleatoric noise) barely applies since ARC-AGI-3 games are deterministic — in deterministic environments, ensemble disagreement is an unusually clean EIG proxy. DreamerV3-XP's *reward-head* disagreement suggests a Tofy variant: keep an ensemble of goal/progress heads and plan toward states where candidate goal hypotheses disagree.

## D. Bayesian goal inference + planning against the posterior

**VariBAD** ([arXiv 1910.08348](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08348), Zintgraf et al., ICLR 2020) — canonical: VAE infers a latent task posterior online; policy conditions on (state, belief), approximating Bayes-optimal behavior in a BAMDP — information gathering about the unknown goal emerges from reward maximization, no explicit EIG term. Evidence: near Bayes-optimal exploration on gridworld/MuJoCo meta-RL; better test-time efficiency than RL².

**MAMBA** (Rimon et al., 2024; TMLR 12/2025 follow-ups, [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=3CWyTh4hJ4)) — model-based VariBAD: belief inference integrated into Dreamer-style latent imagination — i.e., a latent world model that plans under task uncertainty. This is architecturally the nearest neighbor to "JEPA + latent goal posterior."

**Zero-shot RL / reward-free then infer** (Forward-Backward reps, Touati & Ollivier; 2024–26: [BFM-Zero, arXiv 2511.04131](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.04131), Proto Successor Measure [arXiv 2411.19418](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.19418)) — pretrain reward-free, then recover an optimal policy for any test-time reward by regression from a few labeled samples. Mismatch for ARC-AGI-3: it assumes the reward is *given* at test time, not hidden; but the pattern "explore reward-free, then plug an inferred goal into a pretrained representation" could combine with a goal-inference module.

**Active information-theoretic IRL** ([arXiv 2501.00381](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.00381), Bajgar et al., NeurIPS 2024 BDU workshop) — EIG over a reward posterior to select which trajectories to query; gridworld-only, early-stage. Relevant formalism (EIG over *goal* hypotheses rather than dynamics), weak evidence.

Also the inverse direction (inferring goals of *other* agents): SIPS ([arXiv 2006.07532](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.07532)) and open-ended goal inference ([arXiv 2407.16770](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.16770)) — particle filters over goal hypotheses; useful for the *representation* of a goal posterior (particles over goal predicates) even though the observer setting differs.

**Feasibility for Tofy:** a VariBAD-style belief latent is feasible at 1M params, but Bayes-optimality is only ever *implicit* — meta-training across many games is needed for information-seeking to emerge, and ARC-AGI-3 games are adversarially novel. The literature's implicit lesson (and AutumnBench's explicit one): with truly novel rules, explicit hypothesis scoring beats amortized belief policies. Favors the user's explicit falsification design over pure meta-RL.

## E. ARC-AGI-3 specifically (2025–26)

- Official: [ARC-AGI-3 paper, arXiv 2603.24621](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24621v1); [launch blog](https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-agi-3-launch). Humans ~100%, frontier LLM agents 0.5–1% at preview launch; benchmark explicitly frames the task as explore → build a theory of dynamics → *infer what success means*; scoring penalizes wasted actions (human-relative action efficiency).
- **Preview competition (Aug 2025)**: winner **StochasticGoose** (Dries Smit / Tufa Labs, 12.58%, 18 levels; [code](https://github.com/DriesSmit/ARC3-solution)): a CNN trained online with (state, action) → frame-changed labels; hierarchical action/coordinate sampling by convolution; 200K-hash experience buffer. I.e., the winner's whole objective was a degenerate one-bit information measure: "prefer actions predicted to change the frame." No goal inference at all — and it still won. Ceiling of this approach is low (12.58%).
- 3rd place lineage: **Graph-Based Exploration** ([arXiv 2512.24156](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.24156), Rudakov, Shock, Cowley, AAAI 2026 workshop): segmentation + state-transition graph; pick shortest path to untested state-action pairs — systematic novelty search, no explicit hypotheses; median 30/52 levels, far above frontier LLM agents.
- **2026 Milestone Prize #1** ([blog](https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-prize-2026-milestone-1)): 1st "The Duck" (Tufa Labs) — LLM writing Python in a live REPL; 2nd/3rd — vision-LLM-as-policy.
- **Executable World Models for ARC-AGI-3** ([arXiv 2605.05138](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05138v1), Rodionov, May 2026): coding agent maintains one executable Python world model, verifies against all observed transitions, refactors toward simplicity (MDL proxy), plans through it; GPT-5.5 version fully solves 15/25 public games, mean human-relative action efficiency 58.12%.
- **OPINE-World** (Section B): 20/25 games, action-efficiency 78.4 — current best published, and the one using explicitly Bayesian uncertainty-directed exploration.

**Pattern:** every strong ARC-AGI-3 result is either (a) LLM + executable-code hypothesis maintenance with verification against observed transitions, or (b) sub-symbolic novelty/change-seeking exploration. Nobody has published a small-model agent doing *explicit hypothesis discrimination*; the gap between (b)-tier (~12–30% territory) and (a)-tier (58–78% efficiency) is precisely hypothesis-structured modeling + targeted uncertainty reduction — the niche Tofy's planning-by-falsification targets.

## Evidence-quality summary

- Rigorous regret/efficiency-vs-oracle evidence: BED lineage (DAD/Step-DAD: EIG vs oracle posteriors), MaxInfoRL (bandit regret), PTS-BE (formal IG guarantees + sparse-reward wins).
- Sample-efficiency-to-identify-rules evidence: WorldCoder (interactions to competence vs deep RL), EMPA (human-matched learning curves), WorldLLM (transition-coverage vs oracle policies, and explicit ablation showing low-likelihood-seeking > learning-progress > random), AutumnBench (human–model gap localized to exploration/belief updating).
- Benchmark-native evidence: OPINE-World and Rodionov report the actual ARC-AGI-3 action-efficiency metric; competition leaderboards give random/greedy baselines implicitly (frontier LLM agents ≈ 0.5–1%).
- Weakest links: empowerment (no strong 2024–26 planning-cost result), active IRL (gridworld workshop stage).

## Feasibility verdict for Tofy

The compute-feasible synthesis of the literature: K-head JEPA ensemble (or K parametric goal-hypothesis heads) → candidate-action scoring by rollout disagreement (Plan2Explore/PTS-BE, clean here because games are deterministic) → mixed with posterior-weighted goal value (EFE/MaxInfoRL-style trade-off) → hypotheses pruned by observed-transition likelihood, with Thompson-style allocation over surviving hypotheses (CWM-GGP). All components run in well under 0.1 s at 1M params for tens of candidate actions; the ~4000-action space needs a learned proposal head (the amortization lesson from DAD, and exactly what StochasticGoose's change-prediction CNN already validated on this benchmark). Explicit-EIG scoring of all 4000 actions per step would be the only budget risk; the literature's uniform answer is amortize the proposal, score only the shortlist.
