# Findings: raising changed-pixel exact accuracy in Tofy

## Direction 1 — Recurrent depth for spatial propagation (high priority; this is where the evidence points)

**Headline evidence.** The ARC Prize team's ablation of HRM ([The Hidden Drivers of HRM's Performance on ARC-AGI](https://arcprize.org/blog/hrm-analysis), 2025) found the hierarchy was nearly irrelevant: a same-size transformer matched HRM, and the **outer refinement loop (temporal depth — repeated application of the same computation with the answer re-fed) did the heavy lifting**. A mechanistic follow-up ([arXiv 2601.10679](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10679v2), 2026) frames HRM as latent search that scales with iteration count. TRM ([Less is More: Recursive Reasoning with Tiny Networks, arXiv 2510.04871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04871), 2025) distilled this: a single 2-layer, 7M net, recursed many times with deep supervision, hits 45% ARC-AGI-1. This is the closest published relative to Tofy and directly supports the "4 steps is far too few" diagnosis.

**Caution from 2026 follow-ups:** [TRM on ARC-AGI-1: Inductive Biases, Identity Conditioning, and Test-Time Compute (arXiv 2512.11847)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11847) shows TRM's accuracy saturates after few latent updates (shallow *effective* recursion) and depends on puzzle-ID conditioning plus 1000-sample voting. Lesson: depth alone doesn't help unless training *forces* the extra steps to be used — hence deep supervision at every step, not just the last.

### Concrete ideas, ranked

**1. NCA-style deep weight-tied conv recurrence with per-step supervision (established; do this first).**
Replace 2×2 inner/outer steps with the same conv cell unrolled 16–64 steps (≥ grid diameter). Key training recipe from [Neural Cellular Automata for ARC-AGI (Xu & Miikkulainen, arXiv 2506.15746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15746), ALIFE 2025; ~10K params/task, 13.4% exact solves): (a) **constant supervision — loss at every time step**, (b) LayerNorm inside the cell, (c) stochastic per-cell update masking (p∈[0,0.75]) for robustness, (d) full-state output s_{t+1}=f(s_t) rather than residual deltas. Their spiral task generalized a locally-learned rule to 100×100 — exactly the propagation regime Tofy fails at. For Tofy, supervise intermediate steps against the *final* target latent (or decoded pixels) with a discounted weight so early steps learn partial propagation. **Candle cost: trivial** — a Rust loop over existing conv ops. Memory of BPTT through 64 steps at 64×64×128 is the constraint on the A40; detach every k steps (truncated BPTT) or use TRM's trick (backprop only through the last few recursions, detach earlier ones). Risk at 560K params: gradient degradation over long unrolls — mitigated by supervision at every step; also training-cost inflation ~16×.

**2. Fix NCA's known long-range failure with one cheap global op per step (established components, novel-ish combination).**
Xu & Miikkulainen explicitly report NCAs "struggled significantly with tasks that required global coordination or propagation of information across longer distances" with purely local kernels. [ARC-NCA (arXiv 2505.08778)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08778) (best 12.9% single-model, 17.6% union; test-time training) got its wins partly from a global propagation channel (GenePropCA). For Tofy: add per-step global-mean-pool → MLP → broadcast (or one 1×1-projected single-head attention over a downsampled grid) inside the recurrent cell. Cost: a few thousand params, standard candle ops. This converts O(diameter) propagation to O(log) for global facts (e.g., "something moved") while local conv handles the wavefront. Risk: global channel can shortcut/collapse; keep it low-dimensional.

**3. TRM/Geiping-style randomized depth + test-time depth extrapolation (established).**
[Geiping et al., Scaling up Test-Time Compute with Latent Reasoning (arXiv 2502.05171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05171), NeurIPS 2025; follow-up "Scaling Latent Reasoning via Looped Language Models", 2026) train with recurrence counts *sampled* per batch (log-normal), then run more iterations at test. For Tofy: sample unroll depth 8–48 during training, run 64 at eval — eval remains a cheap forward pass, satisfying your no-search constraint. Also adopt TRM's **outer answer-refinement**: decode a predicted next-state, re-embed it with the latent, and refine 2–3 times with supervision each round (this is the component ARC Prize found did the heavy lifting). Cost: small; it reuses the encoder/decoder you have.

**4. Skip DEQ implicit differentiation (recommendation against).**
DEQ ([arXiv 1909.01377](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.01377)) needs custom implicit-gradient VJP solves — awkward in candle, and HRM's 1-step gradient approximation (the cheap DEQ surrogate) was exactly the theoretically-shaky piece; TRM dropped it for plain backprop through the last recursion block and improved. Unrolled weight-tied recurrence + detach + deep supervision dominates on implementation cost and published results. ACT/PonderNet-style adaptive halting: low priority — fixed generous depth with per-step supervision gets the benefit without halting-head instability at this scale.

## Direction 2 — Object-centric world models (low priority; evidence is against, for your metric)

**Slot pipeline (Slot Attention [2006.15055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15055) → SlotFormer [2210.05861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05861) → [SlotSSM, NeurIPS 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12272) / [Parallelized Spatiotemporal Binding, arXiv 2402.17077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17077), ICML 2024).** These improve *coarse* object motion and training stability/parallelism, but 2025–26 analyses document persistent failure modes fatal to exact per-pixel grids: slots binding to background while foreground drifts between slots, identity switching, deformation under rotation, and inability to represent fine detail within one slot embedding ([Rethinking Temporal Consistency in Video OC Learning, arXiv 2605.03650](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03650v1); [Temporally Consistent OC Learning by Contrasting Slots, arXiv 2412.14295](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.14295), CVPR 2025). Alpha-mask mixture decoders are soft/blurry — poison for changed-pixel *exact* match. SlotFormer is also unconditional; action conditioning needs PlaySlot ([2502.07600](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.07600))/COMPAS-style additions. No published slot model targets discrete 16-color exact prediction; making that work at 560K–5M params would be a novel, high-risk project (slot-count selection per game, binding collapse with 2–3-color sprites on flat backgrounds).

**Object-centric ARC work is symbolic, not neural-slot.** 2025–26 solvers parse objects for program synthesis: Vector Symbolic Algebras ([arXiv 2511.08747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08747), 3.0% ARC-AGI-1-eval), ILP with object DSLs ([arXiv 2405.06399](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06399)). For ARC-AGI-3 specifically, [Executable World Models for ARC-AGI-3 (arXiv 2605.05138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05138), 2026) has an LLM coding agent maintain a verified Python world model (58% mean per-game score with GPT-5.5). That's a different paradigm than Tofy, but its success confirms ARC-AGI-3 dynamics are short *programs* dominated by propagation/iteration — supporting the depth hypothesis rather than a representational (slot) deficit. ([ARC-AGI-3 benchmark paper: arXiv 2603.24621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24621).)

**Verdict:** don't add a slot bottleneck. If you want object-ness, get it implicitly: the deep recurrent conv + global channel can learn connected-component-style computations without discrete slots.

## Recommended order
1. Deep weight-tied unroll (16–64 steps) + per-step supervision + truncated/last-k backprop — established, near-zero candle cost.
2. Add cheap global channel per step — established components, addresses NCA's documented long-range failure.
3. Randomized train depth, extended eval depth + TRM-style outer answer refinement — established.
4. Slot/object-centric — defer; evidence against at this scale and metric.
5. DEQ implicit diff — skip.
