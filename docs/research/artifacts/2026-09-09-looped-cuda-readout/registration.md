Floating-point ordering and unseen episode performance require empirical checks; the finite C10 fitting-margin mass bound does not extend to new queries by proof. A fixed-coordinate/no-role representation remains a counterexample to inferring learned role selection from oracle pooling alone. Initial-core success cannot receive C7 training credit.
