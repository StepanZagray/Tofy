## Claim and weakest uncertain premise

C18 passes finite action selection at source ec6ba27c: 256/256 fit and 128/128
renamed held cases across 16 types. Action routing and output symmetry impose
renaming transfer, so this is not independent semantic generalization. C15
initial selectors locate roles on reused images. Neither result establishes
actual fresh native image-to-action execution. The empirical question is
whether those frozen components compose correctly on the fixed new panel.

The tensor coordinate formula and its gradients can be derived directly:
positions are attention-weighted public cell coordinates; support effects are
after-agent minus before-agent; query displacement is goal minus agent. CPU
one-hot, soft-attention, control, malformed-input and analytic-gradient tests
already pass for native_binding.rs. This proves/checks local arithmetic under
its assumptions, not fresh visual selection or correct actions. No convergence
or general-architecture theorem is claimed and no paper-faithful label applies.
